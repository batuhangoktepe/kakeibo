Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

In short: Your financial data never leaves your device. We don't collect, store, or share any of your personal information. The app sends anonymous usage statistics to help us improve it, and you can turn them off in Settings.

1. Who We Are

Kakeibo is developed by Halil Göktepe ("we", "us", "our"). This privacy policy explains how the Kakeibo mobile application handles your information.

2. Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. All information you enter into Kakeibo — including transactions, amounts, budgets, savings goals, notes, journal entries, and preferences — is stored exclusively on your device. The only information that leaves your device is the anonymous usage statistics described in section 5, which never include anything you enter.

3. Data Storage

  • Local only: All your financial data is stored on your device and never transmitted to any server.
  • No cloud sync: We do not currently offer cloud synchronization. Your data exists only on the device where you entered it.
  • No accounts: Kakeibo does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information.

4. Third-Party Services

Kakeibo uses the following third-party services:

  • RevenueCat: Used solely to process in-app subscriptions (Kakeibo Pro). RevenueCat receives only the information necessary to manage your subscription through Apple's App Store. We do not share any of your financial data with RevenueCat. You can review RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • TelemetryDeck: A privacy-focused analytics service based in Germany that receives the anonymous usage statistics described in section 5. It receives no personal data and no financial data. You can review TelemetryDeck's Privacy Policy.

5. Analytics and Tracking

We do not use tracking or advertising frameworks, and we never track you across other apps or websites.

Anonymous usage statistics (iOS 2.3.1 and Android 1.3.1 and later). To find out where Kakeibo is confusing, slow, or broken, the app sends anonymous usage statistics through TelemetryDeck:

  • How the app is used: for example, that an onboarding step was completed, a subscription screen was shown, or an expense was recorded (without its amount or note).
  • Technical details: app version, operating system version, device model, language, and region.
  • Stability: freezes, crashes, and errors, as counts and error codes, including summaries provided by the operating system (Apple MetricKit on iOS, app exit reasons on Android).
  • A random identifier that is hashed on your device and cannot be linked to your identity.

Never sent: amounts, income, budgets, savings goals, notes, or anything else you type.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings > Privacy > Share anonymous usage statistics. When it is off, nothing is sent.

6. Notifications

Kakeibo may send local notifications, such as daily spending reminders or the question about a purchase described below, if you enable these features. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and are not sent through any external server.

Pay and log (optional, iOS 2.4 and Android 1.4 and later)

If you turn on Pay and log, Kakeibo can write down your card payments for you and ask which pillar each one belongs to. It is off until you set it up, and you can turn it off at any time.

  • On iPhone: you create a Shortcuts automation that runs after an Apple Pay payment. The automation passes only the amount and the shop name to Kakeibo.
  • On Android: you grant Kakeibo notification access in Android settings. Kakeibo then reads only payment notifications from Google Wallet (and Google Pay), and takes only the amount and the shop name from them. Notifications from every other app are ignored and never stored. You can revoke notification access at any time in Android settings or from Kakeibo's Settings.
  • Stays on your device: the amount and shop name are kept on your device like any expense you type in. They are never sent to us or to any third party, and no bank login or account is involved.

7. Data Export

You can export your transaction data as a CSV file at any time through the app's Settings. The exported file is generated locally on your device.

8. Data Deletion

You can permanently delete all your data at any time through Settings > Reset All Data. Since your financial data is stored only on your device, deleting the app also removes all of it. Anonymous usage statistics cannot be linked back to you.

9. Children's Privacy

Kakeibo does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app does not require any personal information to use.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
